# Biorouter manuscript materials

Shareable study materials for the manuscript *Biorouter: An Agentic-AI-powered Integrated Biomedical Research Environment* (Gu, Bellucci and Baranzini). The manuscript refers to them as the "project repository".

| Folder | Content |
|---|---|
| [`case_studies_paper/`](case_studies_paper/) | Prompts and case reports of the two clinical case studies (MS hospitalization and PREVENT validation), and the PREVENT calculator with its handoff skill |

The benchmark questions, grading rubrics, run-level results and execution records, and the security assessment records, are provided with the article as Supplementary Data and Supplementary Methods.

**Privacy.** No patient-level data are included. The case reports contain aggregate results only. The PREVENT calculator uses MOCK reference and calibration data (see `case_studies_paper/README.md`).
