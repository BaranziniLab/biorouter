About this calculator

PREVENT (American Heart Association, 2023) estimates the 10-year risk of total cardiovascular disease (heart attack, stroke or heart failure), atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (heart attack or stroke) and heart failure in adults aged 30–79 without cardiovascular disease, from 11 routine measurements.

Two scales

PREVENT is the published equation. Calibrated (MOCK) keeps its ranking and rescales it with a Cox-type transformation of the logit of the PREVENT risk. In this public version the parameters are MOCK values (about 80% of the PREVENT risk), not the UCSF recalibration, which is not distributed for privacy purposes.

Inputs

Age, sex, systolic blood pressure, total and HDL cholesterol, body-mass index, eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021), diabetes, current smoking, blood-pressure treatment and statin use. Values can be typed in from any source or loaded from UCSF OMOP with the rules of the validation study (time windows, units, codes); each value shows its source and date. PREVENT is computed only within its validated input ranges.

Reference cohort

MOCK data: 20,000 simulated adults aged 30–79 with plausible PREVENT inputs, included as aggregated counts. They are not patients and have no outcomes; the UCSF reference cohort of the case study is not distributed for privacy purposes.

Cohorts

A cohort studied in a Biorouter chat (skill "prevent-calculator-handoff") is sent to the app over a one-time local connection: PREVENT is computed for every member, with exclusions for missing or out-of-range inputs. Describing a cohort inside the app needs the UCSFOMOPAgent extension in the app's session, which Biorouter cannot attach yet when the app is opened from its exported launcher. The demo patient and the demo cohort are simulated, not patient data.

Data and privacy

OMOP is queried with your own UCSFOMOPAgent credentials; the app stores none. Patient values stay on this computer. When recording or sharing the screen, use the demo patient and keep individual patients hidden.

Limitations

One health system; de-identified, date-shifted data; the calibration is cause-specific and ignores the competing risk of death, so it slightly overstates risk; the projection holds every input but age fixed. For research use only, not for clinical decisions.
