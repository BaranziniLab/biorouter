Principles

Three ideas behind Biorouter

01

Private stays
private.

A chat that uses a private model or private data becomes private, and stays private. It is never sent to a model hosted outside your institution, and public chats cannot read it.

02

Share the method,
not the data.

A workflow carries instructions, never records. Each site runs the same workflow on its own data, with its own models, under its own agreements.

How workflows travel →

03

Built for
biomedical research.

Connect clinical warehouses, knowledge graphs, literature and lab systems from the marketplace. Add skills for omics, statistics and writing. Keep what you learn in a knowledge base with a biomedical vocabulary.
